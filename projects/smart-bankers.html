Smart Bankers Institute

Educational Platform UI & Web Design

Web design for an educational banking training institute. The focus was on presenting course and training information clearly, building a structured content hierarchy, and delivering a responsive website aligned with the institute's identity and enrolment goals.

01

Overview

Smart Bankers Institute is an educational institution offering banking and finance training. The web design project focused on creating a clear, trustworthy website that helps prospective students understand what courses are available, what to expect, and how to enrol.

The primary challenge was managing a moderate volume of course and institutional information without creating a cluttered, overwhelming experience. Educational websites often suffer from information overload — this project prioritised structured hierarchy and clear pathways to key actions.

02

Design Goals

The design was guided by four primary objectives:

  • Communicate institutional credibility — the design needed to convey professionalism and trust appropriate for a banking education provider.
  • Present course information clearly — prospective students should be able to find, compare, and understand courses without navigating through multiple pages.
  • Drive enrolment action — clear, accessible calls to action for enquiry and enrolment at every relevant point in the page.
  • Responsive across devices — mobile visitors should receive an equally structured, readable experience.
03

Page Structure & Hierarchy

The homepage follows a structured hierarchy that moves visitors from context (who SBI is and what they offer) to credibility (why enrol here) to action (course browsing and contact):

04

Visual Design & Course Cards

The visual design uses navy blue and white as the primary palette — consistent with financial and educational sector conventions while maintaining a modern, clean aesthetic. The typography uses a clear size hierarchy with strong weight contrast to guide reading order.

Course cards follow a consistent template: category label, course name, key details (duration, mode), and an action button. This consistency allows visitors to scan and compare quickly without reading full descriptions for every option.

Trust & Credibility System

  • Institutional navy color branding (#174A7E)
  • Featured placement statistics and alumni testimonials
  • Verification badges for certified banking courses

Enrolment Flow & Action

  • Sticky call-to-action buttons for course inquiries
  • Direct WhatsApp / Phone quick connect options
  • Streamlined application form requiring only 3 fields
05

Responsive Design

The responsive design adapts the course grid from three columns (desktop) to two (tablet) to single-column (mobile), maintaining card readability at every size. The navigation collapses to a mobile menu, and the hero section reflows to a stacked layout with the enrolment CTA remaining above the fold.

06

Reflection & Takeaways

Working on a live project for a real institution added useful context to design decisions that might otherwise feel abstract. Understanding the actual audience — adults considering a career change or professional development — shaped decisions around credibility, trust signals, and the directness of the enrolment CTA.

  • Progressive Disclosure: Balancing institutional information with visitor scannability by showing key course highlights first, with full details accessible on click.
  • Live Deployment Success: Delivering a fully functional site directly aligned with student enrolment targets and institutional branding goals.
