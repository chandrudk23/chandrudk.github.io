VRV Aircon

B2B HVAC Website Redesign & UX Audit

A structured website redesign for a B2B air conditioning company, beginning with an audit of the existing site to identify friction points and leading to a responsive high-fidelity redesign with formal handoff documentation.

01

Overview

VRV Aircon is a B2B air conditioning company. The existing website had accumulated several usability problems over time: unclear product hierarchy, inconsistent navigation, and a visual design that didn't communicate the company's expertise effectively.

This project followed a structured process: audit the existing experience, identify and prioritise the key friction points, restructure the information architecture, wireframe the key pages, and produce a responsive high-fidelity redesign with handoff documentation.

02

UX Audit

The audit evaluated the existing website against usability heuristics and basic task-completion criteria. The key friction points identified:

  • No clear product hierarchy — product categories and individual products were presented at the same visual level, making navigation confusing for new visitors.
  • Weak navigation structure — the main navigation did not reflect how users would logically approach finding an air conditioning solution (by system type, application, or brand).
  • Poor visual hierarchy on the homepage — too many competing elements without a clear focal point or primary call to action.
  • No responsive adaptation — the site was not optimised for mobile, a significant issue for B2B buyers who increasingly use mobile devices for initial research.
  • Contact and inquiry friction — the process for getting in touch or requesting a quote was not clearly surfaced and required too many steps to reach.
03

Information Architecture

The restructured IA organises the site around user intent rather than product inventory. A B2B buyer typically arrives at the site with a general need (e.g., commercial cooling for a large space) and needs to quickly understand what's available and how to engage.

04

Wireframes

Wireframes were produced in Figma for five key pages: Homepage, Products Category, Product Detail, Services, and Contact. The primary focus was establishing clear visual hierarchy and ensuring the primary CTA (Get a Quote) was accessible without excessive scrolling.

Homepage & Lead Generation Wireframing

  • Focal Point Hero: Positioned key HVAC capabilities and instant quotation CTAs above the fold to capture high-intent B2B visitors.
  • Product Categories: Grouped VRV systems, commercial split units, and chiller plants into scannable visual modules rather than plain text links.

Catalogue & Specification Wireframing

  • Parametric Filters: Designed left-hand filters for capacity, tonnage, and application space (Commercial vs Industrial).
  • Technical Specs Table: Standardized airflow, energy efficiency ratings, and dimension specs into easily comparable rows.
05

Final UI & Responsive Design

The high-fidelity UI uses a clean, professional palette suited to a B2B industrial brand: navy blue as the primary brand colour, white as the dominant background, and light blue-grey for secondary surfaces. The typography system uses strong weight contrast to guide users through content hierarchy.

Responsive guidelines were produced alongside the desktop UI, covering the key layout changes at tablet and mobile breakpoints — particularly how the product navigation adapts from a horizontal filter bar (desktop) to a collapsible side panel or dropdown (mobile).

Desktop Experience

  • Multi-tier navigation bar with instant Quote action
  • Filterable product grid with inline specifications
  • High-contrast lead capture form on all service pages

Mobile Adaptation

  • Collapsible category filter drawer
  • Single-column scannable specs sheet
  • Persistent tap-to-call & quote bar at bottom
06

Design Handoff Specifications

Handoff documentation was prepared for the development team, covering:

  • Colour tokens: Primary Navy (#174A7E), Accent Blue (#4A90E2), Light Surface (#F5FAFF) with strict accessibility contrast ratios.
  • Typography scale: Montserrat size, weight, and line-height scale mapped to H1-H4 and Body tokens.
  • Spacing & Grid: 12-column desktop grid (24px gutter) collapsing to 4-column mobile grid (16px margin).
  • Interactive states: Hover, active, focus, and error states defined for form inputs and primary buttons.
07

Reflection & Takeaways

The audit phase was the most valuable part of this project. Having a structured method for evaluating an existing website — rather than just having an instinctive reaction to it — produced much clearer design decisions downstream. When every design change can be traced back to a specific audit finding, the reasoning is much easier to communicate and defend.

  • Clarity Over Emotion: In B2B HVAC design, buyers prioritize specs, system reliability, and quick quotation over decorative visual fluff.
  • Friction Elimination: Surfacing the "Get a Quote" action prominently on every page reduced the user path to inquiry by 2 steps.
